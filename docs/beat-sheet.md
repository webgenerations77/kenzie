# Beat Sheet: DMB Hampton Coliseum NYE 1996 VR Demo

This is the second by second plan for the VR proof of concept. It covers the midnight countdown and Tripping Billies from the Dave Matthews Band show at Hampton Coliseum on December 31, 1996, for roughly seven minutes of demo time.

It is a template. Almost every cell is empty on purpose, because almost nothing about what happened on stage that night has been confirmed yet. The cells get filled in as footage is reviewed and questions in `docs/open-questions.md` are answered.

## Ground rules

1. **Only confirmed details go in a cell.** A detail is confirmed when it has been seen or heard on a reference source and logged under a question in `docs/open-questions.md`. Put the question ID next to the detail, for example "Balloons begin to fall from nets over the floor (Q-39, Q-40)".
2. **An empty cell means not yet logged.** It does not mean nothing happens.
3. **If a detail is needed but unconfirmed, write "TBD from footage" and the question ID.** If there is no question for it yet, add one to `docs/open-questions.md` first.
4. **Never fill a gap from memory, from another show, or from what seems likely.** That includes who solos when, lighting colors, and what anyone wore.
5. **Timestamps are demo time**, starting at 0:00 when the countdown begins. The offset from the reference recording goes in the source table below once it is chosen.

## What we know so far

These are the only facts currently in hand. Everything else in this document is TBD from footage.

- On circulating tapes, the midnight countdown runs about 0:29 and leads straight into Tripping Billies, which runs about 6:32. That gives a demo of about 7:01. Both figures are approximate and need to be measured on the chosen reference source (Q-04). If they change, re-cut the timeline below.
- Setlist notes say Béla Fleck and the Flecktones and Paul McCandless were on stage for Tripping Billies. Which Flecktones members, and when the guests walked on, are unknown (Q-31, Q-34).
- Setlist notes say balloons dropped at midnight. The exact second, where they fell from, and what they looked like are unknown (Q-39 to Q-42).

## How to fill in each column

- **What the viewer sees:** the main thing in the viewer's field of view from the fixed floor position (Q-15), described plainly. Include anything that should draw their eye.
- **Lighting:** the lighting state or change at that second, including house lights, stage wash, spots, and beams. Only from footage (Q-20 to Q-24).
- **Performers featured:** who is the focus at that moment and what they are doing, such as singing, soloing, walking on, or gesturing. Only from footage or clear audio evidence (Q-25 to Q-37).
- **Audio notes:** what is heard on the reference recording, such as instruments entering, vocals, a lyric line, crowd noise peaks, or tape artifacts to hide.
- **Crowd behavior:** what the audience around the viewer and in the stands is doing (Q-42 to Q-46).

Mark a row in bold (the time cell) when it is a cue that the freelancer must hit exactly, for example midnight or the first note of the song.
