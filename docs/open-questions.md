# Open Questions: DMB Hampton Coliseum NYE 1996 VR Demo

This is the list of everything that has to be answered before the beat sheet (`docs/beat-sheet.md`) can be finalized and before the freelancer (`docs/unreal-freelancer-scope.md`) can build anything that claims to show what happened that night.

## What we actually know right now

We are working from three pieces of information, and nothing else should be treated as fact until it is confirmed:

1. The show is Dave Matthews Band at Hampton Coliseum in Hampton, Virginia, on December 31, 1996.
2. On circulating tapes, the midnight countdown runs about 0:29 and leads straight into Tripping Billies, which runs about 6:32. Together that is roughly 7:01 of demo time.
3. Setlist notes say Béla Fleck and the Flecktones and Paul McCandless were on stage for Tripping Billies, and that balloons dropped at midnight.

Everything else, including who played what, who soloed when, what the lights looked like, what anyone wore, where anyone stood, and how the crowd reacted, is unknown until we see it on footage or in photos.

## How to use this list

Each question has an ID (Q-01, Q-02, and so on). The beat sheet and the freelancer scope point back to these IDs wherever something is marked "TBD from footage". When a question is answered, write the answer, the source (tape name, file name, timestamp within that source), and who confirmed it directly under the question, then change its status to Answered. Do not delete answered questions. They are the record of where each detail came from.

Status values: Open, Partly answered, Answered, Cannot be answered (no footage exists).

When a question cannot be answered from any source, the team needs to decide whether to leave that detail generic and neutral in the build, or cut it. Either way, note the decision under the question.

## A. Source material

**Q-01. What footage exists for this show?** We need to know whether any video exists at all (pro-shot, broadcast, fan-shot camcorder), how much of the countdown and Tripping Billies it covers, and what angles it shows. Status: Open.

**Q-02. Which audio source is the reference?** Circulating tapes differ. We need to pick one specific source (soundboard, audience, broadcast, or a matrix) and record its lineage so every timestamp in the beat sheet refers to the same recording. Status: Open.

**Q-03. Are the countdown and Tripping Billies continuous on the reference source?** The tapes list them as consecutive, but we need to confirm there is no cut, fade, or tape flip between them, and measure any gap. Status: Open.

**Q-04. What are the exact running times on the reference source?** The "about 0:29" and "about 6:32" figures need to be measured to the second (ideally to the frame, if video exists) on the chosen source. The beat sheet timeline will be re-cut to match. Status: Open.

**Q-05. Does the video (if any) line up with the reference audio?** If the best video and the best audio come from different sources, we need a sync offset and a note of any drift or edits. Status: Open.

**Q-06. What still photos exist?** Press photos, fan photos, and published images from this show would help with stage, lighting, clothing, and crowd details that video may not show clearly. Status: Open.
